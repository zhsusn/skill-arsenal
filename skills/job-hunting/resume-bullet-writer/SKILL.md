# Resume Bullet Writer

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when the user wants to:
- Write or improve resume bullet points
- Transform weak descriptions into strong achievements
- Add metrics and quantifiable results
- Find hidden metrics when they say "I don't have numbers"
- Mentions: "improve my bullets", "quantify", "add numbers", "no data", "results-driven"

Also use when you see weak bullets (passive language, no metrics, vague descriptions).

## Core Capabilities

- Transform weak bullets into achievement-focused statements
- Apply STAR/CAR methods and X-Y-Z formula
- Find hidden metrics in any experience
- Estimate numbers when exact data is unavailable
- Use strong action verbs and quantify impact
- Tailor bullets to specific roles/industries

## The Bullet Point Problem

Most resumes list duties instead of achievements:

❌ **Weak:** "Responsible for managing team"
✅ **Strong:** "Led cross-functional team of 12 to deliver $2M product, increasing revenue by 35%"

❌ **Weak:** "Helped with customer service"
✅ **Strong:** "Resolved 50+ customer issues daily, improving satisfaction scores from 3.2 to 4.8/5"

## Core Frameworks

### 1. The X-Y-Z Formula (Google Method)

**Structure:** "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]"

**Example:**
```
❌ BEFORE: "Managed social media accounts"
✅ AFTER: "Grew Instagram following by 250% (5K to 17.5K) by implementing daily content calendar and influencer partnerships"
```

### 2. STAR Method

- **S**ituation: What was the context?
- **T**ask: What needed to be done?
- **A**ction: What did YOU specifically do?
- **R**esult: What was the measurable outcome?

**Condensed example:** "Revitalized underperforming sales team through training program and commission restructure, improving quota attainment from 65% to 92% and generating $1.8M additional revenue"

### 3. CAR Method

- **C**hallenge: What problem existed?
- **A**ction: What did you do about it?
- **R**esult: What happened?

**Example:** "Reduced customer churn by implementing proactive outreach program, retaining 85% of at-risk accounts worth $500K ARR"

## Power Verbs by Category

**Leadership:** Led, Directed, Managed, Spearheaded, Orchestrated, Oversaw, Mentored
**Achievement:** Achieved, Delivered, Exceeded, Surpassed, Secured, Captured
**Growth:** Grew, Increased, Boosted, Expanded, Scaled, Accelerated, Doubled
**Creation:** Created, Developed, Designed, Built, Launched, Pioneered, Established
**Optimization:** Streamlined, Optimized, Enhanced, Improved, Automated, Simplified
**Analysis:** Analyzed, Assessed, Evaluated, Identified, Diagnosed, Forecasted
**Collaboration:** Collaborated, Partnered, Facilitated, Coordinated, Negotiated, Influenced
**Problem-Solving:** Resolved, Solved, Troubleshot, Eliminated, Reduced, Mitigated

## Quantification Strategies

### The Six Metric Categories

**1. Money:** Revenue generated, costs saved, budget managed, deal size
**2. Percentages:** Growth, improvement, efficiency, quality increase
**3. Time:** Speed, frequency, duration, time saved
**4. Scale/Volume:** People, projects, customers, users, transactions
**5. Quality:** Satisfaction scores, error rates, accuracy, compliance, SLA adherence
**6. Frequency:** Per day/week/month, annual totals, report cycles

### Finding Hidden Metrics

Use discovery questions for any experience:

**Scale Questions:**
- How many people/projects/customers?
- What was the budget/revenue involved?
- How large was the team?

**Impact Questions:**
- What changed because of your work?
- What would have happened without you?
- What got better/faster/cheaper?

**Comparison Questions:**
- How was it before vs. after?
- What was the baseline you improved?

**Role-Specific Metrics:**
- **Sales:** Quota attainment, deals closed, pipeline generated, retention rate
- **Marketing:** Leads generated, campaign ROI, engagement rates, conversion rates
- **Customer Service:** Tickets resolved/day, CSAT, response time, first-call resolution
- **Operations:** Efficiency improvements, cost reductions, cycle times, throughput
- **Engineering:** System uptime, performance improvements, bug resolution, deployment frequency
- **Project Management:** Budget sizes, on-time delivery rate, stakeholders managed
- **HR/Admin:** Hiring numbers, time-to-fill, training completion, onboarding efficiency

### Estimation Techniques

When exact numbers are unavailable:

**Conservative Estimate:** Estimate low to maintain credibility
- You think you saved 100 hours/month → say "75+ hours"
- You think growth was 50% → say "~40%"

**Range Estimation:** "Managed team of 8-12" / "Generated $100K-$150K in revenue"

**Minimum Bound:** "Served 100+ customers daily" / "Managed at least 15 concurrent projects"

**Percentage of Activity:** Calculate from known totals
- Company had 1000 customers → you managed 20% → "Managed 200 customer accounts"

**Time-Based Calculation:** Work backwards from frequency
- Met with 5 clients/week × 50 weeks = "Consulted with 250+ clients annually"

**Quantify Inputs if Outputs Unknown:**
- "Conducted 30+ customer interviews"
- "Analyzed 500+ data points"
- "Created 20+ marketing campaigns"

### Quantification Templates

**Before and After:** "Improved [X] from [before] to [after], resulting in [Y]% improvement"
**Scale:** "[Verb] [number] [things], resulting in [impact]"
**Volume + Impact:** "Processed [number] [items] per [period], achieving [quality metric]"
**Comparison:** "Ranked #[X] out of [Y] in [metric], [context]"

### "I Have No Numbers" Solutions

| Situation | Solution | Example |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| "Just one person on a team" | Focus on YOUR contribution | "Contributed 40% of front-end code for product reaching 100K users" |
| "No access to business metrics" | Quantify activities and inputs | "Created 50+ sales presentations and managed pipeline of 200+ prospects" |
| "No measurable outcomes" | Measure the work itself | "Produced 75-page technical documentation reducing onboarding time by 2 weeks" |
| "Results were confidential" | Use percentages or ranges | "Grew revenue by 40%+ year-over-year" |
| "Entry-level with limited impact" | Quantify throughput and accuracy | "Processed 200+ records daily with 99.5% accuracy, exceeding team average by 15%" |

## Common Bullet Writing Mistakes

**Passive Language:** "Was responsible for..." → "Led, Created, Delivered"
**No Metrics:** "Improved website performance" → "Improved load time by 60% (8s to 3.2s)"
**Duties vs. Achievements:** "Managed social media" → "Grew following by 300% (10K to 40K) in 8 months"
**Too Vague:** "Worked with stakeholders" → "Facilitated weekly meetings with 15+ stakeholders across engineering, product, and executive teams"
**Too Long:** Keep bullets to 1-2 lines maximum

## Bullet Writing Process

1. **Start with the weak bullet:** "Managed social media"
2. **Ask clarifying questions:** Platforms? Followers? Growth? Actions? Impact?
3. **Gather information:** Instagram/LinkedIn, 5K → 18K, content calendar + influencers, 200 leads
4. **Apply X-Y-Z formula:** "Grew Instagram and LinkedIn by 260% (5K to 18K) through content strategy and influencer partnerships, generating 200+ leads"
5. **Optimize length:** Prioritize most impressive metrics if too long

## Output Format

When rewriting bullets, provide:

```markdown
## BULLET IMPROVEMENTS

### Original Bullet #1:
"Managed customer accounts"

### Issues:
- Passive language, no metrics, vague

### Improved Version:
"Grew portfolio of 40 enterprise accounts from $2M to $3.5M ARR (75% growth) through quarterly business reviews and proactive upselling"

### Metrics Added:
- Scale: 40 accounts
- Revenue: $2M → $3.5M ARR
- Growth: 75%
- Actions: QBRs, upselling

### Estimation Notes:
- [Metric]: Estimated based on [reasoning]

---
[Continue for each bullet]
```

## Special Situations

**Entry-Level/Recent Graduates:** Focus on academic projects, internships, volunteer work, club leadership with metrics
- Example: "Led university marketing club of 50 members, organizing 8 events attended by 300+ students and securing $10K in sponsorships"

**Career Changers:** Highlight transferable skills, reframe experience, emphasize relevant projects
- Example: "Led cross-functional teams of 15 across operations and customer experience, achieving 100% project completion rate"

**Gaps in Employment:** Fill with freelance work, volunteer achievements, side projects, professional development

## Gotchas

- **Don't fabricate numbers you can't defend in an interview** — use conservative estimates or ranges instead
- **Avoid numbers that reveal confidential information** — use percentages or omit sensitive figures
- **Don't use numbers that make you look bad** — context matters; "resolved 2 tickets/day" is weak without quality context
- **Limit to 2-3 numbers per bullet** — more becomes cluttered and hard to read
- **Every bullet CAN be quantified** — if you think otherwise, you haven't asked the right discovery questions yet
- **Context is required for percentages** — "increased by 300%" without baseline is meaningless; always include "from X to Y"
- **Don't use passive language with metrics** — "Was responsible for team of 12 generating $2M" is weaker than "Led team of 12 to generate $2M"
- **Beware of vanity metrics** — "sent 500 emails" is less impressive than "achieved 25% response rate from 500 outreach emails"

## Implementation Checklist

For each resume bullet:
1. ✅ Replace weak verb with power verb
2. ✅ Add at least one number/metric
3. ✅ Show specific outcome or result
4. ✅ Include context of scale (team, budget, users)
5. ✅ Keep to 1-2 lines maximum
6. ✅ Read aloud to ensure natural flow
7. ✅ Verify relevance to target role
8. ✅ Ensure every number is defensible and adds credibility
